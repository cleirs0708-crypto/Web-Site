Pamantasan ng Cabuyao

Founded: April 16, 2003

Motto: Kapayapaan, Kaunlaran, Paglilingkod

President: Dr. Roberto F. Rañola Jr.

Board Chairperson: Hon. Dennis Felipe C. Hain

History

Pamantasan ng Cabuyao traces its roots to a Laguna State Polytechnic University (LSPU) satellite campus established in 1993.

In 2002, protests led to the termination of its agreement with LSPU.

A new university was then established, modeled after Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Maynila.

The ordinance was passed on April 15, 2003, and signed on April 16, 2003.

The university was inaugurated on July 31, 2003.
